How to Use WorldIntelligence

Map Navigation
Scroll to zoom · Click & drag to pan · Click any marker to see details · Use 2D/3D buttons to switch views
Search
Use the search bar to find military bases, conflict zones, countries, airports and more. Press / to focus it instantly.
Intelligence Layers
Toggle layers in the left sidebar — Military, Nuclear, Conflicts, Infrastructure, Earthquakes and more. Each layer has a count badge.
Live News
Click the ↑ arrow at the bottom to open the Live News drawer. Watch 15 global news channels live.
Watchlist Alerts
Add countries to your Watchlist in the sidebar. You'll get instant toast notifications when news or conflict events match.
Live Ticker
The scrolling bar below the top nav shows live Reuters & AP headlines. Hover to pause · Click to open the article.
Export
Press E or use the ··· menu to export the current map view as a PNG with timestamp watermark.
